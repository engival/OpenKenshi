Retired 2026-11-02 (BQ13 / BQ14 join rules, cpp/src/ksim/world/join_rules.cpp): the sight rule for package-driven characters.

Replaced by: CombatArena::Ctx::joinAllows (combat.cpp) + joins::allows (join_rules.cpp). A character with an AI package now picks a fight only through
the attack goals of its role list (16 / 15 / 143 / 34) or because it is targeted. A plain fighter (no package: duels, tests) still uses this rule.

The rule, as it stood in CombatArena::Ctx::pickTarget (the loop after the "keep the target for a second" test):

	for (Member& other : arena.members_)
	{
		if (other.id == m.id || !usable(other.fighter) || !enemy(f, other.fighter) || !perception::aware(f, other.id))
			continue;
		...nearest wins...
	}

i.e. every non-player character of another team that was aware of an enemy engaged the nearest one (Hub scan range 1500 u). Residents were all team 0,
so every patron within about 1500 u of a bar fight joined (bar_crowd: Mercenaries 9-10 of 10 runs, original 1; Pacifier / Logan / Molly 8-10 of 10,
original 0). Documented in docs/reference/BAR_FIGHT_LOOP.md "bar_crowd: join rules".

Also retired with it: the setTarget flip (K5 markHostile) only for victims of the attacker's own team. It now also flips a package-driven victim of
another team (a bar guard targeted by a bandit lists the bandit's squad, and a bandit targeted by a guard lists the guard's).
