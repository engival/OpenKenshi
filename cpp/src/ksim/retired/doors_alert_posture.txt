The first door slice (2026-10-31) had the town's gates CLOSE on an "Alert": the owner faction's intent was Defend, or a hostile
raid of another faction was under way to the town; the posture was held 6 game-hours after the cause ended, and an `always locks`
gate was then locked for neutral and hostile strangers. It was a PROVISIONAL design (DOORS.md section 4, question 1: "the owner's
Alert closes the gates"), written before item 20.

Contradicted by the measurement (GATES_CARRY_LIVE.md a, DOORS.md section 8): nothing in the original closes a gate. The only
gate task, MAN_THE_GATE (180), has no alert / enemy / time / relation / crime check and never closes; only shopkeeper packages
ever plan close / lock; live, the UC gates stayed open and unlocked through a 3300-frame fight at the gate. A gate that does get
shut (the player's toggle, a scripted SetDoorState) is reopened by its crew: UNLOCK (3.33 s, if the crew's faction owns the
door) then OPEN. Gate state is now: Open by default, explicit overrides (SetDoorState), broken by raiders; the crew reopens.
